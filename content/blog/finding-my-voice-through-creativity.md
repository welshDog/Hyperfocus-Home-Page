Creativity has been a constant thread throughout my life, from building log cabins to crafting digital worlds. Visual storytelling, in particular, has captured my imagination. I approach projects like a filmmaker, playing out each step in my head before I even begin. There’s a thrill in not knowing exactly how things will turn out—in allowing the process to unfold naturally, scene by scene.

My love of game design continues to grow. Whether it’s creating the “Glitching Robot” or imagining the next viral sensation, I find deep satisfaction in building experiences that engage and entertain. Moving away from physical labor hasn’t diminished my creative spirit—it’s simply changed the medium. Where I once used my hands to craft tangible things, I now use my mind to shape digital worlds.

That creative spark still drives me, whether I’m building a man cave, designing a new game, or envisioning the next big idea. Creativity is more than a hobby for me—it’s my way of making sense of the world.
