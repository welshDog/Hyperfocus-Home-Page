OpenAI is a big name in AI research. They've made a cool AI chatbot called ChatGPT. It's been a hit, with 100 million users in just two months. But OpenAI isn't stopping there. They're always working on making ChatGPT better.

ChatGPT Can Surf the Web

One of the big new things is that ChatGPT can now look stuff up on the internet. This is for people who pay for ChatGPT Plus. Before, ChatGPT could only use info up to 2021. Now, it can give answers with the latest info from the web.

ChatGPT Gets More Skills

OpenAI has added plugins to ChatGPT. These let it connect to popular web services like Expedia, Instacart, OpenTable, and Zapier. This means ChatGPT can do more things and is even more useful.

OpenAI Cares About Safety

OpenAI's boss, Sam Altman, says they test all their AI tools a lot before they let people use them. He thinks that as AI gets more powerful, the government might need to check it too.

What's Next? GPT-5

People are excited about the next big thing, GPT-5. Some think it might be smart like a human (that's called AGI). It might come out in December 2023. To use it, you might need to pay for ChatGPT Plus.

Wrapping Up

OpenAI is always working on new things. They want to make AI better and safer. They're leading the way in AI.
