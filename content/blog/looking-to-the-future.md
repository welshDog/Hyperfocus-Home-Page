Writing this memoir has been a journey of reflection, one where I’ve looked back on the twists and turns that have shaped my life. Sharing my story is my way of giving back—to show that it’s okay to struggle, and it’s okay to succeed in ways that defy expectations.

My hope is that my children will take away the lessons I’ve learned along the way. I want them to understand the importance of resilience, of pushing forward when the path is unclear, and of finding joy in the things that make them unique. More than anything, I want them to know that there’s no one “right” way to live a life.

As I look ahead, I feel a sense of peace. I may not know what the future holds, but I’m confident that whatever comes next, I’ll approach it with the same creativity, determination, and openness that have carried me this far.
